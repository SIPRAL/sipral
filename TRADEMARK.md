"Sipral" and the Sipral logo are marks of their owner, named in `AUTHORS`. The
AGPL and the commercial licence grant rights in the code. Neither grants rights
in the name.

## Additional terms under section 7 of the AGPL-3.0

Every file of Sipral conveyed under the AGPL-3.0 carries these additional terms,
as section 7 permits: under 7(e), the licence grants no rights under trademark
law in the name Sipral or its logo; under 7(c), a modified version must not be
presented as Sipral and must be marked as different from the original.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that your product uses Sipral, or is built with Sipral.
- Using the name in documentation, articles, talks and comparisons.
- Keeping the crate, package and module names intact when you depend on Sipral
  unmodified.
- Linking to this repository and to the project site, <https://sipral.org>.

## Requires written permission

- Naming your product, company, domain or social account so that it contains
  "Sipral", or a name close enough to be mistaken for it.
- Publishing a modified build under the name Sipral, on crates.io, NuGet, npm,
  Maven Central or anywhere else.
- Using the logo as your own product's mark, or altering it.
- Suggesting that your product is official, endorsed or certified.

## Forks

Forking is a right the AGPL gives you and this policy does not narrow it. Ship
the fork under a different name. Keep the attribution and the licence headers,
say plainly that it is a fork of Sipral, and do not present it as Sipral.

## The expanded name

Sipral stands for **Session Initiation Protocol Rust Audio Layer**. The expanded
form appears next to the name at the top of the README and in the package
descriptions of `sipral` and `Sipral`. It explains what the name is short for;
the mark is the short name.
